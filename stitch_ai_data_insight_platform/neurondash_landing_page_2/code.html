Frequently Asked Questions

Is my data secure? expand_more
Yes, we use AES-256 encryption at rest and TLS 1.3 in transit. Your data is never used to train our global models unless you explicitly opt-in.
What file types are supported? expand_more
We support .xlsx, .csv, .pdf, .docx, and .txt files. Our AI is specialized in parsing complex tables from within PDFs.
Can I export my dashboards? expand_more
Absolutely. You can export static versions as PDF or SVG, or export the cleaned data back into Excel/CSV format.
Do I need to know how to code? expand_more
No coding required. NeuronDash is built for business users, researchers, and students. If you can type a question, you can use our platform.
